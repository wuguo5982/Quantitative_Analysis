# Methodology used

- **Primary sources only:** SEC 10-K filings and official investor-relations pages.
- **FCF formula:**  
  **FCF = operating cash flow − capex**
- **Growth formula:**  
  **Revenue growth = (current year revenue − prior year revenue) / prior year revenue**
- **Operating margin formula:**  
  **Operating margin = operating income / revenue**
- **Debt level:** reported as the closest directly visible debt figure from the filing or IR snippet; where the snippet did not expose the exact total debt line, I note the ambiguity.
- **Earnings quality:** judged qualitatively from cash conversion, presence of unusual items, and whether operating cash flow appears to support earnings.
- **Units:** USD millions unless otherwise stated.
